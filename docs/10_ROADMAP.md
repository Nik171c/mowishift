# 10_ROADMAP.md

# MowiShift Product Roadmap

**Project:** MowiShift

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Active

---

# 1. Overview

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of the MowiShift platform.

It serves as a strategic guide for development priorities while remaining flexible enough to adapt to customer feedback and business needs.

Roadmap items may change as the project evolves.

---

# 2. Vision

The long-term vision is to build a modern SaaS platform for workforce scheduling and employee management that can be used by manufacturing, seafood processing, logistics, and industrial companies worldwide.

---

# 3. Development Principles

Every release should:

- deliver business value
- improve usability
- reduce manual work
- maintain backward compatibility whenever possible
- improve scalability
- reduce technical debt

# Long-Term Goals

The long-term objective is to establish MowiShift as a leading workforce management platform by providing:

- intuitive user experience
- scalable architecture
- enterprise-grade security
- high performance
- AI-assisted scheduling
- cross-platform support
- long-term maintainability

---

# Success Criteria

The roadmap will be considered successful when MowiShift:

- reduces scheduling time
- improves workforce visibility
- minimizes scheduling conflicts
- increases employee engagement
- supports organizations of different sizes
- scales without major architectural changes

---

# Roadmap Principles

The roadmap should be reviewed regularly.

Priorities may change based on:

- customer feedback
- business goals
- technical improvements
- regulatory requirements
- market demands

Every release should deliver measurable value while preserving the overall architecture and quality of the platform.
