# Project Overview

MowiShift is a modern Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform for workforce scheduling and employee management.

The project is designed to simplify shift planning, improve communication between administrators and employees, and provide complete visibility into workforce operations.

Although the first version is inspired by the needs of seafood processing facilities, the platform is being developed as a scalable solution that can support manufacturing, logistics, food production, and other shift-based industries.

---

# Vision

Build a reliable, scalable, and user-friendly workforce management platform that reduces administrative work while improving operational efficiency.

---

# Mission

Provide businesses with a simple, modern, and efficient way to manage employees, shifts, requests, and workforce communication.

---
